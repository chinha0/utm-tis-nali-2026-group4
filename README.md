- **Company:** TENDZONE
- **Main Focus:** Smart Classroom and Educational Technology

### 🖥️ Smart Education Terminal

The Smart Education Terminal is an integrated classroom management solution designed to combine multiple devices into one centralized system.

It serves as the heart of TENDZONE's Smart Classroom solution, reducing complicated equipment setups and making classroom management easier.

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/smart-education-terminal.jpg" width="80%" alt="TENDZONE Smart Education Terminal"/>
</p>

### ⚙️ Key Features

**1. Centralized Control**

- Manage audio and video systems.
- Control classroom lighting and air conditioning.
- Manage curtains and interactive devices.
- Control multiple classroom functions through one system.

**2. Built-in Audio and Video Processing**

- Integrated audio processor.
- Power amplifier.
- Video matrix.
- Audio and visual management for classroom activities.

**3. Wireless Connectivity**

- Supports wireless microphones.
- Enables wireless presentations.
- Provides flexible device connectivity.

**4. Recording and Streaming**

- Captures lessons in real time.
- Supports playback and hybrid learning.
- Enables remote lesson sharing.

**5. Centralized Management**

- Simplifies system monitoring.
- Reduces maintenance complexity.
- Improves classroom equipment management.

**6. Universal Equipment Support**

- Supports different brands of classroom equipment.
- Provides flexible integration.
- Reduces restrictions when choosing classroom devices.

## 📝 My Personal Reflection

During my interview with Ms Syahirah Adnan, I learned about an innovative smart classroom solution known as the Smart Education Terminal.

This terminal combines multiple devices and allows lecturers to manage classroom facilities such as lighting, displays, microphones and other equipment through a centralized system.

In my opinion, this innovation creates a more convenient and effective teaching environment. It allows lecturers to focus more on delivering lessons while reducing the complexity of managing different classroom devices.

The Smart Education Terminal also demonstrates how Information Systems, networking, audio-video processing and centralized management can be applied in real educational environments.

This experience sparked my interest in Educational Technology and digital transformation. It motivated me to explore how software and emerging technologies can be integrated into physical environments to solve practical problems.

Overall, the NALI 2026 visit and interview sessions allowed me to gain a deeper appreciation of how ICT innovations are developed and implemented. I believe that these real-world experiences provide valuable understanding that cannot be fully obtained through classroom lectures alone.
